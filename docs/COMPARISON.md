# Canopy HR vs BambooHR vs Rippling

How Canopy HR stacks up against the two platforms it's most often compared to.
BambooHR and Rippling entries reflect their positioning as of 2025; Canopy
entries reflect what is actually built and clickable in the
[live demo](https://canopy-hr.vercel.app).

## The short version

- **BambooHR** wins on simplicity — the favorite for small/mid-size teams.
- **Rippling** wins on automation and breadth — HR + IT in one, at the cost of
  setup complexity and quote-only pricing.
- **Canopy HR** is modeled on BambooHR's philosophy (clean, self-explanatory,
  built for SMBs) while borrowing Rippling's best structural ideas — unified
  approvals, timesheet-aware payroll, and auto-provisioned accounts on hire —
  in one codebase with no per-seat pricing at all.

## Where each one wins

**Choose Rippling** if you need HR + IT device management, 500+ integrations,
and payroll automation at scale — and can absorb the setup effort and opaque
pricing.

**Choose BambooHR** if you want the friendliest HR system for a small or
mid-size team and are fine with payroll and contractors being weak spots.

**Canopy HR's pitch**: BambooHR's ease of use with several of the gaps closed —
multi-rate payroll math, contractor support, hire-time account provisioning,
and a single approvals inbox — plus modules BambooHR sells as add-ons
(recognition, training, e-sign) included in one build. Payments, tax filing,
and third-party integrations are intentionally simulated: it's a
demonstration platform, and it's honest about that line.
